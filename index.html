Ancient Heritage • Modern Engineering

Traditional Sekku (Stone Squeezed) Oil Extraction

Sri Lanka is blessed with an ancient culinary food culture that is as old as the nation itself. Since ancient times, Ceylon artisans utilized the granite "Sekkuwa" (stone mortar and pestle mechanism) to squeeze sesame and coconut oils without generating heat friction.

Unlike modern industrial high-heat expellers that scorch delicate nutrients, stone squeezing protects fragile vitamins, sesamol, and healthy fats. The engineers and food experts at Celebration Holdings transformed this ancestral Sekku into a food-grade modern stone squeezer that operates under cleanroom HACCP standards.
