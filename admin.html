Quick Management Guide

• Products Manager: Adjust prices in USD, toggle stock availability (In Stock, Seasonal Harvest, Limited Batch, Out of Stock), and select EU/USDA certifications per product.
• Categories: Add new agricultural ranges (e.g. Herbal Teas, Spices) or remove existing ones.
• Company Blog: Write and publish announcements about foreign exhibitions (BIOFACH Germany, Foodex Japan), organic certifications, or export milestones.
• Automatic Sync: All changes made here immediately update www.chpl.lk without needing any server rebuild.
